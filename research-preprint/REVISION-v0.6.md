## Central argument

Policy authoring is deeply coupled with AI engineering. Teams should start with an explicit moderation boundary and representative labeled evaluation cases, then test policies, model-specific adapters, and decision rules together. A benchmark supports selection; it cannot decide the intended policy or acceptable error costs. New models are candidates in this process, not automatic replacements.
